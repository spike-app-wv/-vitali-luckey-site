How much does grooming cost?

Cost depends on your pet's size, coat type and the condition of the coat. A matted doodle and a short-haired terrier are not the same job, so we quote each one over the phone instead of posting a number that may not apply to you. What to expect

What a grooming
appointment looks like.

01

Drop-off

Bring your pet in and tell us what you want. We talk through the coat and the cut first, so nobody is surprised at pickup.

02

Bath and dry

A warm bath with shampoo suited to the coat, a thorough rinse, and a low-heat dry. The part most dogs pretend to hate.

03

Haircut and nails

A breed-appropriate cut, or whatever length you prefer. Nails, ears and paw pads get sorted while we are at it.

04

Pickup

A finishing tidy, a bow if your dog will tolerate one, and a call or text when they are ready to be admired.
